Hi! I'm Tiasia Saunders.

I’m a data journalist, developer and investigative reporter who analyzes data, builds graphics and tools and creates interactive stories to explain complex issues and hold institutions accountable. My work uses data and technology to uncover patterns, examine systems of power, and help audiences better understand the forces shaping their lives.

My past newsrooms include the Investigative Project on Race and Equity, Capital News Service, the Howard Center for Investigative Journalism and The Washington Informer.

I received a Master of Journalism in Data Journalism from the University of Maryland’s Philip Merrill College of Journalism and a B.A. in Media, Journalism and Film from Howard University.
