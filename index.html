Tiasia Saunders

I’m a data journalist and investigative reporter in Washington, D.C. I analyze data, build graphics and create interactive stories to help readers understand complex issues, especially racial and socioeconomic disparities and the institutions that shape people’s lives.

My past stops include the Investigative Project on Race and Equity, Capital News Service, the Howard Center for Investigative Journalism and The Washington Informer.

I received a Master of Journalism in Data Journalism from the University of Maryland’s Philip Merrill College of Journalism and a B.A. in Media, Journalism and Film from Howard University.
